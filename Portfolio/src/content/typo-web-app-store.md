Typo Web App Store was built as a digital storefront that makes product selection simple and visually engaging. The interface emphasizes a clean browsing flow, easy access to offers, and an overall experience designed for users to explore products without confusion.

My role focused on the frontend experience, including page layout, styling, interaction design, and overall usability. I aimed to keep the platform visually clear while ensuring it felt polished and modern enough for a real online shop environment.

This project strengthened my ability to design and implement user-focused interfaces that are both practical and visually appealing.
