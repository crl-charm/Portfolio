IdeaFlow was designed to support day-to-day business operations through a centralized system for managing staff access, menu items, inventory, and sales performance. The goal was to create a workflow that was easy to understand, practical to use, and capable of helping business owners monitor their operations in real time.

The system includes admin and staff account management, product catalog administration, inventory tracking, sales monitoring, and analytics dashboards. I focused on handling the backend logic, database structure, and system architecture to make sure the app was both functional and organized for real-world use.

This project reflects my interest in building solutions that solve actual business problems while keeping the experience simple, efficient, and scalable.
